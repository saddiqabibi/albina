Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Construction Services

What types of construction projects do you handle?
We handle a wide range of projects including residential homes, commercial buildings, industrial structures, renovations, interior design, and landscaping. Whether you're building from scratch or renovating an existing space, we have the expertise to deliver exceptional results.
How long does a typical construction project take?
Project timelines vary depending on the scope and complexity. A standard residential home typically takes 6-12 months, while smaller renovations can be completed in 2-4 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout the process.
Do you provide free estimates and consultations?
Yes, we offer free consultations and site visits to assess your project requirements. We provide detailed, no-obligation estimates so you can make an informed decision. Contact us today to schedule your free consultation.
What makes Al-Bina Construction different from other companies?
We stand out through our commitment to quality, transparent communication, and customer satisfaction. With 25+ years of experience, we bring expertise, integrity, and attention to detail to every project. Our team ensures that your project is completed on time, within budget, and to the highest standards.
Do you offer warranties on your construction work?
Yes, we provide comprehensive warranties on all our construction work. Our workmanship is backed by a 10-year structural warranty, and we ensure that all materials used meet quality standards. We stand behind our work and are committed to your long-term satisfaction.
Can you help with obtaining construction permits?
Absolutely! We handle all the necessary paperwork and permit applications on your behalf. Our team is well-versed in local building codes and regulations, ensuring that your project is fully compliant and approved by the relevant authorities.
What payment terms do you offer?
We offer flexible payment plans tailored to your budget. Typically, we require an initial deposit to commence work, followed by milestone-based payments throughout the project. We also offer financing options through our partner financial institutions.
How do I get started with my construction project?
Getting started is simple! Contact us via phone, email, or through our website to schedule a free consultation. Our team will visit your site, discuss your vision, and provide a detailed proposal and estimate. We'll guide you through every step of the process.
