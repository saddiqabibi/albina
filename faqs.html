General Questions

1. What services does Al-Bina Construction offer?

Al-Bina Construction offers a comprehensive range of construction services including:

  • Building Construction: Residential, commercial, and industrial buildings
  • House Renovation: Kitchen, bathroom, and complete home makeovers
  • Architecture Design: Modern and innovative architectural designs
  • Interior Design: Residential and commercial interior design
  • Professional Painting: Interior and exterior painting services
  • Maintenance & Support: Ongoing property maintenance

2. How long has Al-Bina Construction been in business?

Al-Bina Construction has been serving clients across Pakistan since 1998. With over 25 years of experience, we have built a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction in the construction industry. We have successfully completed over 500 projects across the country.

3. Where is Al-Bina Construction located?

Our main office is located at Main Boulevard, Lahore, Pakistan. We serve clients across Pakistan, including major cities like Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Multan, Faisalabad, and Peshawar. We also provide services in smaller cities and towns across the country.

4. What are your office hours?

Our office hours are:

  • Monday - Friday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Saturday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
  • Sunday: Closed

For emergency support, we are available 24/7 by phone at +92-300-1234567.

Construction Questions

5. How long does a typical construction project take?

Project timelines vary based on the scope and complexity of the work. Here are some general estimates:

  • Small Renovations: 2-4 weeks
  • Medium Renovations: 1-3 months
  • Single-Storey Home: 6-9 months
  • Double-Storey Home: 9-12 months
  • Commercial Buildings: 12-18 months
  • Large Industrial Projects: 18-24 months

We provide detailed project timelines during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout the construction process.

6. Do you provide free estimates and consultations?

Yes, we offer free consultations and site visits to assess your project requirements. During the consultation, we discuss your vision, budget, and timeline. We then provide a detailed, no-obligation estimate. Contact us today to schedule your free consultation.

7. How do you ensure quality in your construction work?

We ensure quality through multiple measures:

  • Premium Materials: We source materials from trusted, high-quality suppliers
  • Skilled Workers: Our team consists of certified professionals with years of experience
  • Quality Checks: We conduct regular inspections at every stage of construction
  • Industry Standards: We follow international building standards and best practices
  • Customer Feedback: We keep clients informed and involved throughout the process

8. Do you help with obtaining construction permits?

Absolutely! We handle all the necessary paperwork and permit applications on your behalf. Our team is well-versed in local building codes and regulations across Pakistan. We ensure that your project is fully compliant and approved by the relevant authorities, saving you time and hassle.

9. Do you offer warranties on your construction work?

Yes, we provide comprehensive warranties on all our construction work:

  • Structural Warranty: 10-year warranty on structural integrity
  • Workmanship Warranty: 5-year warranty on all workmanship
  • Material Warranty: Varies by material, but we use only quality materials
  • Customer Satisfaction: 100% satisfaction guarantee on all projects

Renovation Questions

10. What type of renovation projects do you handle?

We handle a wide range of renovation projects:

  • Kitchen Renovations: Complete kitchen remodels and upgrades
  • Bathroom Renovations: Modern bathroom designs and installations
  • Room Additions: Adding extra rooms to existing homes
  • Basement Finishing: Converting basements into living spaces
  • Home Makeovers: Complete interior and exterior transformations
  • Commercial Renovations: Office, retail, and restaurant renovations

11. Can you renovate while we are living in the house?

Yes, we can renovate while you are living in the house. We understand the importance of minimizing disruption and will work with you to create a schedule that causes the least inconvenience. We isolate work areas, manage dust and noise, and ensure your living space remains safe and habitable throughout the renovation process.

12. How much does a typical renovation cost?

Renovation costs vary widely based on the scope, size, and materials chosen. Here are some rough estimates:

  • Kitchen Renovation: PKR 500,000 - 2,000,000
  • Bathroom Renovation: PKR 300,000 - 1,000,000
  • Room Addition: PKR 800,000 - 3,000,000
  • Full Home Renovation: PKR 2,000,000 - 10,000,000+

We provide detailed, itemized quotes after a site visit and consultation.

Design Questions

13. Do you offer architecture and interior design services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive architecture and interior design services through our in-house team of certified architects and designers. We create innovative, functional designs that reflect your style and meet your needs. From concept to completion, we handle every aspect of design and planning.

14. Can you provide 3D renderings of my project?

Absolutely! We provide detailed 3D renderings and walkthroughs of your project before construction begins. This allows you to visualize your space, make changes, and ensure everything is exactly as you want it before we start building. Our 3D designs help eliminate surprises and ensure complete satisfaction.

15. How much does design service cost?

Our design services are competitively priced and vary based on the scope of the project. We offer:

  • Free Initial Consultation: No cost to discuss your project
  • Architecture Design: Typically 5-8% of construction cost
  • Interior Design: Based on project scope and square footage
  • Package Deals: Discounted rates for combined design and construction services

We provide transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Pricing & Payment Questions

16. What payment terms do you offer?

We offer flexible payment plans tailored to your budget:

  • Initial Deposit: 25-30% of total project cost to commence work
  • Milestone Payments: Progress-based payments at key project milestones
  • Final Payment: Remaining balance upon project completion
  • Financing: We can connect you with partner financial institutions for project financing

17. How do you calculate construction costs?

We calculate construction costs based on several factors:

  • Square Footage: The size of the structure
  • Materials: The quality and type of materials chosen
  • Labor: The complexity and duration of the work
  • Location: Site accessibility and logistics
  • Design Complexity: Unique architectural features and finishes
  • Permits: Government fees and approvals

We provide detailed, itemized quotes so you understand exactly where your money is going.

18. Are there any hidden costs in your quotes?

No, we believe in complete transparency. Our quotes are detailed and itemized, with all costs clearly explained. We include a 10-15% contingency fund for unexpected expenses and communicate any potential changes promptly. We never add hidden fees and always get your approval before making changes that affect the budget.

Legal Questions

19. Is Al-Bina Construction licensed and insured?

Yes, Al-Bina Construction is fully licensed and insured to operate across Pakistan. We comply with all local building codes and regulations. Our insurance covers:

  • General Liability: Protection against property damage and injuries
  • Worker's Compensation: Coverage for our workers
  • Professional Liability: Coverage for design and professional services
  • Property Insurance: Coverage for materials and equipment

20. What contracts and agreements do you provide?

We provide comprehensive, legally-binding contracts that protect both parties. Our contracts include:

  • Scope of Work: Detailed description of work to be performed
  • Timeline: Project start and completion dates
  • Payment Schedule: Detailed payment terms and milestones
  • Warranties: Clear warranty terms and conditions
  • Dispute Resolution: Process for resolving any issues
  • Change Orders: Procedure for project changes

We recommend you review all contracts carefully and consult a legal professional if needed.
