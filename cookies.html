Welcome to Al-Bina Construction's Cookies Policy! This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website. We are committed to being transparent about our use of cookies and giving you control over your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

  • How They Work: Cookies are stored by your browser and contain information that helps websites remember your preferences and activity.
  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device for a set period and are used to remember your preferences for future visits.
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by the website you are visiting (Al-Bina Construction).
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by other websites or services that we use (like Google Analytics).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for various purposes to enhance your experience on our website:

  • Essential Functions: To enable core website functionality and navigation.
  • Performance Monitoring: To analyze how visitors use our website and improve its performance.
  • Remembering Preferences: To remember your settings, language preferences, and saved items.
  • Marketing & Advertising: To deliver relevant advertisements and measure their effectiveness.
  • Security: To help protect our website and users from security risks.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies on our website:

3.1 Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas of the website. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

  • Purpose: Core website functionality
  • Storage Duration: Session-based
  • Examples: Session IDs, security tokens

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies help us remember your preferences and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

  • Purpose: Remember user preferences
  • Storage Duration: Up to 1 year
  • Examples: Language preferences, saved settings

3.3 Analytics / Performance Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site.

  • Purpose: Website analytics and performance
  • Storage Duration: Up to 26 months
  • Examples: Google Analytics cookies

3.4 Advertising / Targeting Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you and your interests. They also help us measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

  • Purpose: Targeted advertising
  • Storage Duration: Up to 2 years
  • Examples: Google AdSense cookies

6. Managing Cookies

You have the right to control how cookies are used on your device. Here are the ways you can manage cookies:

6.1 Browser Settings

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Site Settings → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Site Permissions → Cookies and Site Data

6.2 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies.

6.3 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out Extension
  • Google Ad Settings
  • NAI Opt-Out Page

7. Your Choices

You have the following choices regarding cookies:

  • Accept All: Accept all cookies and continue with full functionality.
  • Reject Non-Essential: Reject all cookies except necessary ones.
  • Custom Preferences: Select which types of cookies you want to allow.
  • Change Later: Change your cookie preferences at any time.

Note: Disabling cookies may affect the functionality and performance of our website. Some features may not work as expected.
